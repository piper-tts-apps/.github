# Privacy Policy

**For the apps published by Piper TTS Apps**

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Our apps do not collect personal information, usage data, or analytics, and contain no advertising or tracking. Text you enter or send to an app for speech is processed entirely on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Our apps connect to the internet only when you browse, download, or preview voices. Those requests fetch the voice catalog, voice files, and short voice samples from Hugging Face (huggingface.co); if a sample is unavailable there, an app may fetch it from the Piper community samples site (rhasspy.github.io). As with any web request, those hosting services receive standard technical information such as your device's IP address and handle it under their own privacy policies. We do not receive, store, or have access to that information.

Voices you download, settings, and audio files you export are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the voice or uninstall the app.

Questions: piper-feedback@ihor-shevchuk.dev - https://ihor-shevchuk.dev

This policy applies to any app published by Piper TTS Apps that links to it, including Piper for iOS, macOS, and Android.

# Piper TTS Apps

Private, offline neural text-to-speech for Apple and Android devices, powered by [Piper](https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl).

---

### 📱 Apps
- **Piper for iOS & macOS** — [piper-app-apple](https://github.com/piper-tts-apps/piper-app-apple) — system-wide voices for VoiceOver and Spoken Content
- **Piper for Android** — [piper-app-android](https://github.com/piper-tts-apps/piper-app-android) — system TextToSpeech service

### 🧩 Libraries & tooling
- [piper-objc](https://github.com/piper-tts-apps/piper-objc) · [piper-kotlin](https://github.com/piper-tts-apps/piper-kotlin) · [piper1-gpl-spm](https://github.com/piper-tts-apps/piper1-gpl-spm)
- [piper1-quantization](https://github.com/piper-tts-apps/piper1-quantization) — quantized voice catalogs (FP16 / INT8) for on-device use, published on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/IhorShevchuk/piper1-voices-fp16-quantized)

Everything runs fully on device — no accounts, no cloud, your text never leaves your device.
